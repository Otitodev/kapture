## Bundled backdrop images

The editor's "style export" option ships with image backdrops in `public/backdrops/`.

**Designs.** `tidepool`, `ember`, `orchid`, `meadow`, `dune`, `glacier`, `neon-dusk`, `signal`, `topo` and `cobalt` are original Kapture artwork, licensed with the rest of the project under Apache-2.0.

**Artworks.** The `art-*` images are public-domain works from the [Art Institute of Chicago open-access collection](https://www.artic.edu/open-access). Each was marked `is_public_domain` by the museum's API when downloaded. They are cropped to 16:10 and re-encoded as WebP. Attribution is given as a courtesy.
