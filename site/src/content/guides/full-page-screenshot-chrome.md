title: How to take a full-page screenshot in Chrome
description: Capture an entire web page, not just what is visible, in one image with Kapture. One shortcut, no account, and the image stays on your device.
order: 1
---

Chrome has no built-in button for capturing a whole page. Kapture scrolls the page for you and stitches the result into a single image.

## Steps

1. Open the page you want to capture.
2. Press `Ctrl + Shift + 3` (`Cmd + Shift + 3` on Mac), or open the Kapture popup and choose full page.
3. Wait while Kapture scrolls the page. The popup shows progress when it is open.
4. The finished image opens in the editor, where you can crop or annotate it.

The capture is also saved to your [library](/docs/capture-modes/), so you can find it later by title or site.

## Other ways to capture the same page

- **Visible area** (`Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + 1`) saves only what is on screen.
- **Selected region** (`Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + 2`) lets you drag over just the part you need.
- **Single element** lets you hover any element and click to capture it.

## Limits

Chrome does not let any extension capture internal pages such as `chrome://` pages or the Chrome Web Store. Kapture shows a clear message instead of failing silently. If the shortcut does nothing, check `chrome://extensions/shortcuts` for conflicts.

## Where the image goes

Nothing is uploaded. The screenshot is stored in your browser profile until you copy, download or delete it. See [privacy](/docs/privacy/) for details.
