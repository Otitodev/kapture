Kapture is a free Chrome extension for recording a browser tab or your screen. This page lists what you get and what you do not.

## What you get

- **No account and no sign-in.** Install it and record.
- **No watermark** on recordings or screenshots.
- **Local storage.** Recordings are saved in your browser profile, not uploaded to a server.
- **Tab or screen recording,** with tab audio and optional microphone audio.
- **MP4 or WebM output,** depending on what your browser supports.
- **Screenshots and an annotation editor** in the same extension.

## What to expect

- Each recording is limited to **five minutes**.
- There is **no sharing link**. You export the file and share it yourself.
- Effects such as automatic zoom or styled backgrounds are not available yet.
- Recording works in Chrome on desktop. Chrome blocks capture of internal pages such as `chrome://`.

## Start recording

1. [Install Kapture](/docs/install/).
2. Press `Ctrl + Shift + 5` (`Cmd + Shift + 5` on Mac) to record the current tab, or use the popup to record your screen.
3. Click **stop** on the in-page bar when you are finished.

Step-by-step details are in the guide to [recording a tab with audio](/guides/record-tab-with-audio-chrome/). How your data is handled is explained on the [privacy page](/docs/privacy/).
