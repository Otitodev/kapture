Kapture can record the tab you are on, including the audio playing in it, and add your voice from the microphone if you choose.

## Steps

1. Open the tab you want to record.
2. Press `Ctrl + Shift + 5` (`Cmd + Shift + 5` on Mac), or choose record tab in the Kapture popup.
3. Turn on the microphone switch in the popup first if you want to narrate. It is off by default.
4. A small bar appears on the page with a timer, a **stop** button and **discard**.
5. Click **stop** when you are done. The recording opens in the editor and is saved to your library.

## What to know

- **Length:** a single recording is limited to five minutes.
- **Format:** MP4 when your browser supports it, otherwise WebM.
- **Audio:** tab audio is recorded from the tab you chose. Microphone audio is only added when the microphone switch is on, and Chrome may ask for permission.
- **Recording the whole screen:** choose record screen in the popup to capture a window or your full screen. Chrome shows its own picker for that.
- **Stopping from Chrome:** if you press Chrome's own "Stop sharing" control, Kapture saves what was recorded so far.

## Privacy

The video never leaves your device unless you export it. Temporary recording pieces are removed once the recording is saved or discarded. Read the [privacy details](/docs/privacy/).

Chrome does not allow tab recording on internal pages such as `chrome://` pages.
